From report to resolution

How it works

Three steps, and you always know where your report stands.

  1. Submit

    Choose a category, describe the problem in writing, say where it is, and add a photo if you have one.

  2. Review

    The administrators review your report, assign it, or send you a text or email if they need more details.

  3. Resolve

    Follow the status with your reference number until the issue is resolved.

No phone call needed

  • Report in writing

    Everything is done through a form. You never have to speak to anyone to make a report.

  • Updates by text or email

    Choose how we reach you. Status changes are also shown on the Track page.

  • Help in person

    Visiting the HOA office? Ask for a sign language interpreter or written back-and-forth.

Spotted something that needs attention?

It takes about two minutes. For urgent hazards, go to the HOA office or contact emergency services.
