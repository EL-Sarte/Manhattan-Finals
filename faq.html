Common questions

Who can submit a report?

Residents and property owners aged 18 and above can report issues in their residential community.

Do I need to make a phone call?

No. Reporting and tracking are done in writing on this website. You choose to receive updates by text message, by email, or in person at the HOA office.

Can I ask for a sign language interpreter?

Yes. Tick the interpreter box on the report form. The administrators will arrange an interpreter or written communication for any in-person visit.

Do I need an account?

No. Your name and mobile number are enough, so the administrators can reach you if they need more details.

What can I report?

Maintenance problems, pests, documentation concerns, and community-member concerns such as noise or parking. Pick the closest category on the report form.

What happens after I submit?

You get a reference number right away. The administrators review the report, and you can follow its status on the Track page.

What if the administrators need more information?

The status changes to Needs more information and a note explains what is missing. Processing continues once you reply.

What if someone already reported the same issue?

The report is marked as a duplicate and linked to the original, so no second work order is created.

Is this a real reporting system?

No. HomeLink is a front-end prototype for a class project. Reports stay in your browser and the sample cases are demonstration data.

Who do I contact in an emergency?

Go to the HOA office or contact local emergency services directly. Do not wait for a report to be reviewed when there is immediate danger.

What the statuses mean

Submitted
We received your report.
Under review
An administrator is checking the details.
Needs more information
We need something from you before we can continue.
In progress
The issue is assigned and work has started.
Resolved
The issue is fixed and the report is closed.
Rejected
The report cannot be acted on. The reason is shown on it.
Duplicate
The same issue was already reported and is being handled.
